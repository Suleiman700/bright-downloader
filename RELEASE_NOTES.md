## Bright Downloader 1.0.0

The first release of Bright Downloader, a fast, free and open-source download manager for Windows.

- **Faster downloads**: each file is split across up to 32 connections, and connections that finish early take over work from slower ones.
- **Pause and resume** any time, even after a crash or a reboot.
- **Video downloads**: HLS and DASH streams, plus 1,000+ video sites through yt-dlp, in the quality you choose.
- **Browser extension** for Chrome, Edge and Firefox that hands downloads and videos to the app.
- **Queues, scheduler, speed limits**, checksums, automatic retries, site grabber and batch downloads.
- **Automatic updates** that are checked against published SHA-256 sums before they install.
- English, Arabic, French and Spanish.

### Downloads

- `Bright-Downloader_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe`: installer (no administrator rights needed, updates itself).
- `Bright-Downloader_1.0.0_x64-portable.zip`: portable version. Unzip anywhere and run `brightdl.exe`; settings stay in the `data` folder next to it.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums for both files.

Requires Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
