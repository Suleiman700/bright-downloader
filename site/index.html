Everything a download manager should do

The features you'd expect from a classic download accelerator, with nothing to pay and no ads.

Many connections per file

Up to 32 connections. When one finishes, it takes over half of the biggest part that's left, so no connection sits idle.

Pause and resume

Stop a download whenever you like and continue it later, even after a crash or a reboot. Changed files on the server are detected.

Videos from web pages

The browser extension puts a download button on videos, including HLS and DASH streams and sites supported by yt-dlp.

Queues and scheduler

Start downloads at night, stop them in the morning, and shut down or sleep the PC when everything is done.

Checked and safe

Checksums for your downloads, an optional virus scan of each finished file, and updates that are verified before they install.

In your language

English, Arabic, French and Spanish. Speed limits, proxies, saved logins, link grabber and batch downloads are all included.

Get started in a minute

No account, no sign-up.

  1. Download and installRun the installer; it doesn't need administrator rights.
  2. Add the browser extensionDownloads and videos from Chrome, Edge or Firefox go straight to Bright Downloader.
  3. DownloadPaste a link or click one in your browser. New versions install themselves when you agree.
